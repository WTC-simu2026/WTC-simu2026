## Records requested

1. Original machine-readable time channel for each declared specimen.
2. Original unfiltered load-channel record, including each piezoelectric load-washer channel if recorded separately.
3. Actuator displacement channel.
4. Gauge-section strain channel.
5. Grip-end strain channel used for load-signal alignment.
6. Corrected load/stress and strain records used for the published curves and scalar properties.
7. Sampling rate, channel units, sign conventions, calibration factors and calibration records.
8. Per-specimen t1/t2 alignment windows, filters, smoothing, baseline correction and data-reduction code or worksheet.
9. Original specimen dimensions, cross-sectional area, gauge length, orientation, extraction location and test temperature.
10. Per-specimen elastic-modulus estimate, uncertainty budget, rejected/repeated test notes and any record-retention or release restriction metadata.

## Delivery and record status

- Preferred format: Native machine-readable files when available; otherwise CSV or TSV with a data dictionary.
- Preferred delivery: Electronic delivery.
- Partial release: Release any segregable non-exempt portion and identify unavailable, destroyed, transferred or withheld records by category.
- Fees: No fee commitment is made by this local draft; request advance notice before any charge.

This request seeks measurement and processing records, not a new technical analysis or an interpretation of the World Trade Center event.
